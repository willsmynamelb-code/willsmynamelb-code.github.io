Featured book · Original T.Y.M.E.

A year of reflection. A life of possibility.

T.Y.M.E. — Today You Mean Everything by William Meyers is a collection of 365 daily reflections for a more present, purposeful, and grounded life.

Across seven connected themes, the book invites readers to make peace with themselves, meet suffering with compassion, deepen relationships, and bring everyday choices closer to their values.

Each reading pairs reflection with a practical exercise and questions to consider. Publication and ordering information will be added when confirmed.

In development · The sober companion

A different book for the recovery journey.

Recovery can begin at a kitchen table, on a bunk, outside a meeting, or on the first morning after a difficult night. This book meets readers wherever they are.

Across 365 daily entries, A Sober Companion is being shaped as a guided sitting: a short reflection, a practice, and questions to carry through the day and into tomorrow.

It draws on the Twelve Steps while welcoming people who are new to recovery, returning after a setback, or finding their own path.

THE BOOK'S STRUCTURE

Twelve Steps as a monthly lens

Each month explores one Step through the ordinary moments of recovery.

January begins with honesty. In the months that follow, each Step shapes the daily reflections and practices in its own way.

The Step guides the month without turning each page into a lecture. The entries are meant to feel like distinct, quiet sittings: grounded in daily life, honest about difficulty, and open to readers with different beliefs and recovery paths.

A daily entry is planned to include

A short reflection, a practice, a question to ask yourself, a thought to carry with you, and a question looking toward tomorrow.

Our mission

Today is where change begins.

At the heart of T.Y.M.E. is a simple conviction: the present moment is the only one we can live. The past can teach us, and the future can offer hope, but today is where our choices take shape.

We don't deny the past. We learn from it. We don't ignore the future. We prepare for it. But we refuse to sacrifice today to either one.

Our hope is to meet people navigating addiction and recovery with dignity, honest reflection, and practical encouragement. The books are an early expression of that purpose.

About the founder

A purpose shaped by lived experience.

T.Y.M.E. was founded by William Lee Meyers. His personal journey through addiction and mental health challenges helped shape a vision of recovery grounded in dignity, presence, and hope.

His books include the original T.Y.M.E. — Today You Mean Everything and the developing T.Y.M.E. Sober Companion, written under the author name William Meyers.
