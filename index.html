Today You Mean Everything

The Present Moment Matters

At the heart of T.Y.M.E. is a spiritual truth: The present moment is the only moment we have been given to live.

We cannot return to yesterday, and we cannot step into tomorrow before it arrives. The past can teach us, and the future can give us hope, but today is where life is actually happening.

Science & Spirit

From a scientific perspective, our conscious experience unfolds moment by moment. We remember what has already happened and imagine what may happen next, but our thoughts, choices, relationships, and experiences can only occur in the present.

Philosophy has recognized this same truth in many different forms: a meaningful life requires our attention to what is actually before us rather than becoming trapped in regret or consumed by anxiety.

The Sacred Present

We believe the present moment can be sacred. It is where we can become still enough to recognize God's presence. It is where we can quiet the noise of regret, fear, anxiety, and distraction and reconnect with something greater than ourselves.

Healing Through Presence

The past may contain our pain, but it does not have to define us. The future may contain uncertainty, but it does not have to control us.

Healing begins when we return to the moment we are actually in.

In the Present, We Can...

  • Breathe
  • Forgive
  • Pray
  • Choose differently
  • Love
  • Begin again

Our Commitment

✓ We don't deny the past. We learn from it.

✓ We don't ignore the future. We prepare for it.

✓ We refuse to sacrifice today to either one.

Yesterday is no longer ours to live. Tomorrow is not yet ours to live. Today is the gift we have right now.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Recovery & Support Services

🏠

Sober Living Homes

Safe, structured environments designed to support sustained recovery. Our homes provide community, accountability, and therapeutic peer support for individuals committed to sobriety.

  • Safe housing
  • Peer mentorship
  • Structure & routine
  • Community connection
💚

Mental Health Support

Holistic mental health services grounded in compassion and evidence-based practices. We address trauma, anxiety, depression, and co-occurring disorders with dignity and hope.

  • Individual counseling
  • Group therapy
  • Crisis support
  • Trauma-informed care
🤝

Peer Support Specialists

Trained peer specialists who have walked the path of recovery. We offer authentic understanding, shared experience, and hope to those struggling with addiction and mental health challenges.

  • Peer mentoring
  • Crisis intervention
  • Recovery coaching
  • Relapse prevention
🎯

Life Skills Training

Practical tools for building a sustainable recovery lifestyle. We teach financial literacy, job readiness, healthy relationships, and wellness practices for long-term success.

  • Employment prep
  • Financial wellness
  • Communication skills
  • Self-care practices
👥

Family Support

Recovery affects entire families. We offer resources, education, and counseling to help loved ones understand addiction, set healthy boundaries, and heal alongside their family members.

  • Family counseling
  • Education programs
  • Support groups
  • Healing resources
🌱

Wellness & Spiritual Growth

Holistic recovery includes mind, body, and spirit. We integrate mindfulness, meditation, spiritual exploration, and wellness activities to support whole-person healing and transformation.

  • Mindfulness training
  • Spiritual direction
  • Wellness activities
  • Creative expression

About the Founder

William Lee Meyers

A Personal Journey to Service

William Lee Meyers founded T.Y.M.E. from a deep personal understanding of the struggle with addiction and mental health. His journey through recovery shaped his commitment to creating a nonprofit that honors the sacred nature of healing and the power of presence.

With professional training in peer support and a genuine passion for helping others, William has dedicated himself to building comprehensive recovery services that integrate spiritual wisdom, scientific evidence, and authentic human connection.

Vision for T.Y.M.E.

William envisions T.Y.M.E. as a beacon of hope for individuals and families navigating recovery. By combining evidence-based practices with spiritual wisdom and peer support, he's building an organization that transforms lives through the simple but profound truth that the present moment is where healing happens.
