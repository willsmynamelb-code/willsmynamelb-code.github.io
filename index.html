Our Mission

Healing begins in the present moment.

The Present Moment Matters

At the heart of T.Y.M.E. is a spiritual truth: the present moment is the only moment we have been given to live.

We cannot return to yesterday, and we cannot step into tomorrow before it arrives. The past can teach us, and the future can give us hope, but today is where life is happening right now.

Science & Spirit

Our awareness unfolds moment by moment. We remember the past, anticipate the future, but our real experience is always in the present.

T.Y.M.E. honors both scientific understanding and spiritual wisdom: healing happens when we slow down, become present, and move forward with purpose.

Healing Through Presence

The past may contain pain. The future may contain uncertainty. But neither has to define us.

Healing begins when we return to the moment we are actually in.

In the Present, We Can...

  • Breathe
  • Forgive
  • Pray
  • Choose differently
  • Love
  • Begin again

Our Vision

A future where healing is accessible, human, and hopeful.

✦

What we believe

We believe recovery is possible for everyone who is willing to take the next step. We believe compassion, accountability, and community can help people restore their lives and reclaim their future.

✧

What we want to create

We want to build a future where sober living, peer support, wellness care, and compassionate guidance are available to those who need them most.

✶

What guides us

We are guided by the belief that every person deserves dignity, opportunity, and a chance to heal—without shame, without judgment, and without losing hope.

Peer Support

Grounded in lived experience, peer support provides encouragement, accountability, and dependable connection during the recovery process.

Life Skills & Stability

We help individuals build healthy routines, practical life skills, confidence, and long-term recovery habits.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Care

🌿

Wellness & Spiritual Growth

Recovery includes the whole person—mind, body, and spirit. We support healing through reflection, prayer, mindfulness, and wellness practices.

Project Spotlight

T.Y.M.E. Companion

Your personal wellness companion in the palm of your hand

A wellness companion for recovery and reflection

T.Y.M.E. Companion is designed to be your personal guide through mindfulness, emotional grounding, and intentional reflection. It creates a safe, judgment-free space where you can reconnect with yourself, track your wellness journey, and build a stronger foundation for recovery.

🧘 Guided mindfulness & reflection
📓 Personal wellness journal
🎯 Weekly intention setting

Currently under development

About the Founder

William Lee Meyers

Rooted in recovery and service
