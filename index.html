Our Mission

At the heart of T.Y.M.E. is a spiritual truth:

The present moment is the only moment we have been given to live.

We cannot return to yesterday, and we cannot step into tomorrow before it arrives. The past can teach us, and the future can give us hope, but today is where life is actually happening.

Three Ways We Understand This

Science

Our conscious experience unfolds moment by moment. We remember what has already happened and imagine what may happen next, but our thoughts, choices, relationships, and experiences can only occur in the present.

Philosophy

A meaningful life requires our attention to what is actually before us rather than allowing ourselves to become permanently trapped in what was or consumed by what might be.

Spirit

The present is where we can become still enough to recognize something greater than ourselves. It is where we can quiet the noise and reconnect.

But T.Y.M.E. goes beyond simply being present.

We believe the present moment can be sacred.

The past may contain our pain, but it does not have to define us. The future may contain uncertainty, but it does not have to control us.

In the Present, We Can

  • Breathe
  • Forgive
  • Pray
  • Choose differently
  • Love
  • Begin again

Our commitment

We don't deny the past. We learn from it.
We don't ignore the future. We prepare for it.
But we refuse to sacrifice today to either one.

Yesterday is no longer ours to live.
Tomorrow is not yet ours to live.
Today is the gift we have right now.

And that is why:
Today You Mean Everything.
