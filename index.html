Our Mission

Healing begins in the present moment.

The Present Moment Matters

At the heart of T.Y.M.E. is a spiritual truth: the present moment is the only moment we have been given to live.

We cannot return to yesterday, and we cannot step into tomorrow before it arrives. The past can teach us, and the future can give us hope, but today is where life is happening right now.

Science & Spirit

Our awareness unfolds moment by moment. We remember the past, anticipate the future, but our real experience is always in the present.

T.Y.M.E. honors both scientific understanding and spiritual wisdom: healing happens when we slow down, become present, and move forward with purpose.

Healing Through Presence

The past may contain pain. The future may contain uncertainty. But neither has to define us.

Healing begins when we return to the moment we are actually in.

In the Present, We Can...

  • Breathe
  • Forgive
  • Pray
  • Choose differently
  • Love
  • Begin again

What We Offer

Support designed for real recovery

🏠

Sober Living Support

Safe, structured recovery housing and peer-driven accountability for individuals rebuilding stability and sobriety.

💚

Mental Health Support

Compassionate care for addiction, trauma, anxiety, depression, grief, and emotional overwhelm through trauma-informed services.

🤝

Peer Support

Guided by lived experience, peer support creates connection, encouragement, and accountability for those navigating recovery.

🎯

Life Skills & Stability

Recovery is more than abstinence. We help individuals build structure, responsibility, communication, and long-term wellness habits.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Support

Recovery affects the whole family. We provide education, understanding, and support for loved ones walking through healing with a loved one.

🌱

Wellness & Spiritual Growth

Mindfulness, prayer, reflection, and whole-person healing help restore balance in the body, mind, and spirit.

Project Spotlight

T.Y.M.E. Companion

A wellness companion for recovery and reflection

T.Y.M.E. Companion is a companion app created to support mindfulness, emotional grounding, weekly intention setting, and personal reflection. It complements the mission of T.Y.M.E. by helping people reconnect with themselves in the present moment.

This app is an important project in development and a natural extension of the T.Y.M.E. mission, while T.Y.M.E. itself remains the broader recovery and nonprofit initiative.

About the Founder

William Lee Meyers

Rooted in recovery and service

William Lee Meyers founded T.Y.M.E. after a deep personal journey through the realities of addiction, mental health struggles, and the need for compassionate, practical support.

His mission is to build a nonprofit environment where people can feel seen, supported, and equipped to heal with dignity, structure, and hope.
