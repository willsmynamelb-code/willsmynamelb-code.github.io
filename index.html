JANUARY 1 · STEP ONE

You Have Arrived

Sometimes a new beginning looks just like an ordinary morning.

Sunlight crosses the kitchen table. Outside, a truck shifts gears and moves on. Everything looks familiar, but this day has never been lived.

You may have called this day one before. You may be tired of promises, especially your own. Yet your willingness to try today matters.

Place your feet on the floor. Feel what holds you steady. Take one unhurried breath. You are here.

Step One begins with honesty about what addiction has cost and what you haven't been able to control. The truth may ache, but seeing it clearly is not the same as condemning yourself. It gives you somewhere real to begin.

Outside, the morning continues, and you can meet it one honest step at a time.

Today's Practice

Find one quiet minute. Notice your feet on the floor. Name one truth about your recovery, then choose one small way to honor it.

Ask Yourself

What truth could help me take my next step?

The purpose behind T.Y.M.E.

Today is where change begins.

We cannot change yesterday, but we can choose how we meet today. T.Y.M.E. brings honest reflection, responsibility, compassion, and connection to people navigating recovery and everyday life.

We don't deny the past. We learn from it. We don't ignore the future. We prepare for it.

Looking ahead

The Vision

My vision for T.Y.M.E. (Today You Mean Everything) is to help create a world where no one is defined by their past, their struggles, or their mistakes.

T.Y.M.E. began with books inspired by lived experience and a simple belief: meaningful change can begin today. But the vision extends far beyond any one book or one person's story.

We all have a story to tell. The lessons we gain through our hardest experiences may be exactly what someone else needs to find their way forward.

I think of it as passing a flashlight to someone in the dark. We cannot walk their path for them, but we can help them see the next step. If sharing my story brings hope to even one person, it will have been worth telling. And when others share what they have learned, that light reaches farther.

My hope is to grow T.Y.M.E. into a compassionate, recovery-centered community offering encouragement, connection, and practical resources. Over time, I envision partnerships with qualified professionals and organizations providing counseling, therapy, sober living, and eventually licensed treatment and rehabilitation services.

These are goals for the future. Today, our work begins with books, reflection, and connections to independent resources.

Because your past does not define your future, and today, you mean everything.

William MeyersFounder, T.Y.M.E.
