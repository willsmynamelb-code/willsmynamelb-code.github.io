Our Mission

Healing begins in the present moment.

The Present Moment Matters

At the heart of T.Y.M.E. is a spiritual truth: the present moment is the only moment we have been given to live.

We cannot return to yesterday, and we cannot step into tomorrow before it arrives. The past can teach us, and the future can give us hope, but today is where life is happening right now.

Science & Spirit

Our awareness unfolds moment by moment. We remember the past, anticipate the future, but our real experience is always in the present.

T.Y.M.E. will honor both scientific understanding and spiritual wisdom: healing happens when we slow down, become present, and move forward with purpose.

Healing Through Presence

The past may contain pain. The future may contain uncertainty. But neither has to define us.

Healing begins when we return to the moment we are actually in.

Planned Programs

Services in development

🏠

Sober Living

We are designing structured, supportive recovery housing to help individuals stay accountable while rebuilding stability, purpose, and confidence.

🧠

Mental Health Support

We are developing compassionate care for emotional challenges, trauma, grief, anxiety, and co-occurring needs through trauma-informed approaches.

🤝

Peer Support

We are building peer-led support grounded in lived experience, offering encouragement, accountability, and nonjudgmental connection.

🎯

Life Skills & Stability

We are creating resources to help individuals build healthy routines, practical life skills, confidence, and long-term recovery foundations.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Care

We are developing family support to help loved ones heal together, communicate more healthily, and understand the recovery journey.

🌿

Wellness & Spiritual Growth

We are designing integrated wellness support through reflection, prayer, mindfulness, and holistic practices that honor mind, body, and spirit.

Project Spotlight

T.Y.M.E. Companion

A wellness companion app in active development

A wellness companion for reflection and grounding

T.Y.M.E. Companion is being developed as a supportive tool to help individuals practice mindfulness, emotional grounding, and intentional reflection. It will create a safe, nonjudgmental space to reconnect with the present moment and support personal wellness.

🧘 Guided mindfulness & reflection
📓 Personal wellness journal
🎯 Weekly intention setting

Under active development

This companion app is an extension of the T.Y.M.E. vision. We are building an accessible tool to help people stay grounded and connected to their wellness journey.

About the Founder

Rooted in recovery and service
