A Sober Companion

T.Y.M.E. — Today You Mean Everything: A Sober Companion by William Meyers offers 365 daily recovery reflections organized around twelve monthly themes inspired by Twelve Step principles.

Grounded in compassion, honesty, and personal responsibility, the readings invite a daily moment of reflection and purposeful action.

Every entry closes with Today's Practice, Ask Yourself, and Carry This With You. A topical Reflection Finder helps readers locate readings for moments when they need a particular kind of encouragement or perspective.

Publication status: The manuscript is complete and a 381-page paperback production candidate has been prepared. Physical proof approval and Amazon KDP Live publication are not yet verified. A purchase link will be added when publication is confirmed.

Our mission

Today is where change begins.

At the heart of T.Y.M.E. is a simple conviction: the present moment is the only one we can live. The past can teach us, and the future can offer hope, but today is where our choices take shape.

We don't deny the past. We learn from it. We don't ignore the future. We prepare for it. But we refuse to sacrifice today to either one.

Our hope is to meet people navigating addiction and recovery with dignity, honest reflection, and practical encouragement. The book is an early expression of that purpose.

Presence

About the founder

A purpose shaped by lived experience.

T.Y.M.E. was founded by William Lee Meyers. His personal journey through addiction and mental health challenges helped shape a vision of recovery grounded in dignity, presence, and hope.

He writes under the author name William Meyers and is the author of T.Y.M.E. — Today You Mean Everything: A Sober Companion. The manuscript is complete and paperback publication is under review.
